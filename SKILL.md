# XXD Panel 027 · 东方纸张压印浮雕

Turn each supplied photograph into one finished Eastern museum poster embossed into heavy ivory paper. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must retain the same source's identity, silhouette, pose, and narrative relation while reducing it to shallow raised and recessed relief, fine incised lines, quiet same-tone shadows, and a very small matte-gold focus when earned. Choose top-down, frontal, slight-top-down, or axial extension from the source itself. The result must feel like precise paper sculpture and exhibition graphics—not folded origami, white plaster, or a generic 3D emboss effect.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; an explicitly confirmed whole-canvas ratio or exact resolution before generation; single complete-canvas raster generation by default; high-fidelity source reference in paired modes; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; deterministic composition as fallback only; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections refine aesthetics and copy but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes target a visually equal 50/50 relationship within one coherent generated canvas. Minor generative deviation is acceptable unless the user explicitly requires pixel-exact halves; exact deterministic geometry belongs to the documented fallback. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- The whole final canvas ratio or exact pixels must be explicitly resolved before generation. Offer the archived/original-prompt 3:4, source aspect as an explicit choice, common ratios, or custom ratio/pixels; never infer explicitly selected final-canvas dimensions silently. Exact pixels take precedence over an explicitly chosen ratio.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve source identity through at least three specific cues while using shallow embossing, debossing, incised contour, stepped paper height, edge shadow, and same-tone value changes to build one dominant relief. Source-related environmental contours may support depth and recognition but never become a competing diorama. It is not literal photo tracing, folded origami, colourful papercraft, white plaster sculpture, glossy metal, a generic logo emboss, cheap bevel filter, ecommerce mockup, or unrelated relief icon.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 027 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve at least three identity, silhouette, pose, action, structure, scale, opening, negative-shape, or relation cues → choose a top-down, frontal, slight-top-down, or source-axis view from the subject's own structure → compress detail into shallow raised relief, recessed paper, fine incised lines, stepped planes, and source-grounded environmental contours → organise one dominant relief through central or source-earned axis, clear primary/secondary depth, generous ivory paper, and museum-like stillness → keep the palette within ivory, warm white, or pale paper tones → place matte gold only on the single most important recognition point when it earns emphasis → reveal height, edge, paper fibre, and delicate shadow with soft warm light → align one short title and sparse microtype with the relief axis, base, or whitespace edge**.

The relief map comes from the source's actual silhouette, pose, action, opening, overlap, functional structure, relation, and environment—not from attaching generic seals, clouds, fans, waves, auspicious ornaments, or stock bas-relief motifs. Chinese embossing, paper sculpture, seal-impression restraint, museum catalogues, Eastern whitespace, and precise exhibition graphics are material and compositional influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the relief silhouette, height map, view axis, environmental contour, gold focus, or title alignment. Also reject literal miniature dioramas, full-colour illustration, white plaster or porcelain, deep architectural carving, ornate gilding, glossy metal, heavy drop shadow, exaggerated bevel, smooth CGI, hard product lighting, flat line-art substitution, and templated luxury packaging.

## 027 visual system

### Source identity through relief abstraction

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across silhouette, pose/orientation, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, mass, scale, relation distance, or dominant direction.
- Translate identity into a relief map: which contour becomes a raised edge, which volume becomes a shallow plane, which opening becomes a recess, which overlap becomes a stepped height, which small feature becomes an incised line, and which relation becomes a shared relief boundary.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific horizon, terrain, or spatial relation.
- Preserve recognition at a glance without recreating every photographic detail. Every raised plane, recess, and line must clarify identity, action, depth, or relation.

### Museum composition and source-earned view

- Read the source's centre of gravity, contour trajectory, depth order, symmetry, movement, and functional axis. Choose top-down, frontal, slight-top-down, or axial extension according to what best preserves its identity; never impose one viewpoint on every subject.
- Use a calm central axis or a clearly source-earned offset axis, disciplined scale, shallow foreground/background steps, and large paper silence to organise one focal relief.
- Preserve generous ivory negative space as museum breathing room. Only a few source-related environmental outlines may extend the axis or establish depth; decorative scene building is forbidden.
- Recompose for each aspect ratio rather than simply centring or cropping the same relief. Keep essential identity cues, gold focus, title, and depth transitions inside safe regions.

### Emboss, deboss, incise, and step

- Use low raised relief for principal masses, shallow recesses for openings and negative shapes, fine incised lines for essential small structure, and restrained stepped planes for depth.
- Relief height remains physically plausible for thick cotton or mould-made paper. Edges are clean but soft, with delicate contact shadows; nothing looks separately glued on or deeply carved from stone.
- Use a few broad relief levels plus only the fine marks required for recognition. Avoid noisy micro-embossing, ornate borders, arbitrary motifs, and relief detail that erases the silhouette.
- Maintain sculptural presence without becoming a miniature replica. It should look pressed and sculpted into premium paper, not rendered with a generic 3D displacement map.

### Ivory paper, quiet light, and matte-gold focus

- Keep the field and relief within one family of heavy ivory, warm white, cream, or very pale paper. Depth comes from height, edge shadow, and tiny value shifts—not from a multicolour palette.
- If the source has one decisive recognition point, translate only that point into a very small matte-gold line, foil-like detail, or warm highlight. Gold is optional, never broad, glossy, ornamental, or a second subject.
- Illuminate with soft warm diffuse light that reveals paper fibre, pressed height, incised edge, layer step, and delicate natural shadow while preserving the near-monochrome calm.
- Reject dirty ageing, grey concrete, chalk, plaster, porcelain, glossy lamination, metallic reflection, deep black shadows, hard spotlight, low-poly facets, and cheap card-stock appearance.

## Copy and typography belong to 027

027 text is a restrained exhibition structure aligned with the relief—not a detached advertising headline, decorative pseudo-classical label, extruded word, or generic caption block.

- Automatic copy derives one short source-specific word or title from identity, theme, action, emotion, relation, or supported symbolism, plus only a very small number of factual or compositional micro-notes when useful.
- Dates, places, provenance, and factual numbers must be supplied or reliably established. Abstract index numbers must visibly read as compositional indexes rather than facts.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 027 construction:

- **Role:** an exhibition label and compositional axis that clarifies identity, place, action, or symbolic meaning while remaining clearly readable.
- **Voice:** concise, quiet, precise, contemplative, and source-specific; an aphoristic line is allowed only when its insight is visibly earned by the source.
- **Construction:** one short title plus zero to three subordinate keywords, indexes, or micro-notes in deep grey or restrained matte gold; subtle printing, foil, debossing, or fine serif form only.
- **Placement:** align with the central/source axis, directly beneath the relief, along a whitespace edge, or with one structural contour. Type must share the relief's order rather than float as a header.
- **Material:** fine printed, lightly debossed, or restrained matte-gold type on paper; never glossy metal, chunky extrusion, foam lettering, or illegible sculptural text.
- **Script:** choose a native high-editorial serif or refined text face for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin proportions, joining, direction, spacing, and legibility rather than forcing one Latin typographic manner onto every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.
